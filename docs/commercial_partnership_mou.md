# Commercial Partnership Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
## Distribution of CareFlow as an Integrated Software-and-Services Oncology Navigation Bundle

**Effective Date:** November 1, 2026  
**Parties:**  
1. **CareFlow Systems, Inc.** ("Technology Provider" / Developer of the CareFlow Screening Orchestration Platform)  
2. **National Oncology Navigation Alliance (NONA) / Regional Cancer Care Partners** ("Navigation Partner" / Commercial Provider of Certified Oncology Patient Navigators)

---

## 1. Purpose & Strategic Alignment

In modern cancer care, technology alone fails if clinical navigation teams are understaffed, and navigation teams fail if burdened by fragmented spreadsheets and manual EHR chart hunting. 

This Memorandum of Understanding formalizes a strategic commercial partnership to market, distribute, and operationalize **CareFlow Navigation-as-a-Service (NaaS)**: an integrated, turnkey bundle combining CareFlow's deterministic workflow software with NONA's credentialed, trained oncology nurse navigators.

---

## 2. Integrated Solution Bundle ("CareFlow Complete")

Under this commercial agreement, participating health systems and accountable care organizations (ACOs) purchase an all-inclusive solution covering:
1. **Software Platform:** CareFlow enterprise deployment with SQLite WAL / PostgreSQL persistence, SMART on FHIR Epic Hyperspace embedding, and automated outbox SMS outreach.
2. **Dedicated Clinical Navigation Capacity:** 1.0 FTE certified oncology nurse navigator per 1,500 annual abnormal screening findings, managing cases directly in CareFlow.
3. **Clinical Governance & Oversight:** Dual supervisor sign-off protocols, multilingual translation review (Hindi, Bengali, Spanish), and malpractice liability reduction tracking.

### Shared Savings / Value-Based Care Incentive
In health systems participating in Medicare Shared Savings Programs (MSSP) or commercial ACO risk contracts, the partnership retains **15% of verified net cost savings** generated from early-stage cancer diagnosis down-staging (derived from the CareFlow HEOR model).

---

## 4. Operational Division of Responsibilities

### CareFlow Systems Responsibilities:
- Host and maintain the CareFlow platform with 99.9% uptime SLA.
- Provide bi-directional EHR integration (FHIR R4 appointment sync and Epic Hyperspace activity embedding).
- Maintain HIPAA BAA compliance, column-level envelope encryption, and automated security monitoring.
- Provide continuous software updates, offline LLM evaluation benchmark monitoring, and quarterly HEOR financial reporting.

### Navigation Partner Responsibilities:
- Recruit, credential, and supervise licensed RN / Oncology Certified Nurses (OCN) assigned to client health systems.
- Ensure all assigned navigators undergo rigorous CareFlow platform onboarding and achieve dual supervisor sign-off certification.
- Fulfill daily clinical SLA targets: 100% of abnormal screening findings contacted within 48 hours; 0 cases orphaned without active owner.

---

## 5. Performance Indicators & Quality Commitments

The partnership guarantees the following contractual outcomes to client health systems:
1. **Lost-to-Follow-Up Reduction:** Reduce screening lost-to-follow-up rates from the baseline 25–35% to **$\le 5.0\%$** across the cohort.
2. **Time to Tissue Diagnosis:** Decrease median elapsed days from abnormal screening finding to tissue biopsy from 42 days to **$\le 14$ days**.
3. **Patient Engagement:** Achieve $\ge 90\%$ patient contact acknowledgement rate within 72 hours of screening result.

---

## 6. Execution & Term

This MOU establishes a binding commercial collaboration for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, renewable automatically upon mutual satisfaction of pilot clinical benchmarks.
